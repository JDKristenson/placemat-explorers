A window to the weather

Optional. Without a forecast, the page invites your child to look outside and make observations.

Use a city, not your address. Search sends this city and your IP address to Open-Meteo. Fetching a forecast sends the selected city's coordinates. Your child's age is never sent.

Privacy, printing & weather

All activities are generated locally, using a small library of age-banded prompts. There is no AI service, analytics, tracking, location permission or automatic printing.

No settings are saved by this app. Your browser may retain form entries, print files, or history. Printed and saved pages can include the city you choose, but never the age.

Weather is a forecast, not a safety alert. Check local official warnings with a grown-up. Weather service access needs an internet connection.
